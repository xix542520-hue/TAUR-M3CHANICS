# TAUR DETAILING — SERVICE CATALOG v1.0

## Purpose

This catalog defines detailing services as production units before pricing is attached.

The rule is:

**DEFINE THE WORK → DEFINE THE STANDARD → MEASURE THE WORK → THEN PRICE IT.**

No service in this catalog requires a fixed customer price yet. Pricing belongs to the quote engine and must be informed by field data.

---

# 1. SERVICE FAMILIES

## A — MAINTENANCE

Routine services intended to maintain an already serviceable vehicle condition.

Examples:
- A1 Maintenance Interior
- A2 Maintenance Exterior
- A3 Maintenance Full

## B — RESTORATION

Services where additional labor is required to materially improve accumulated contamination, staining, buildup, deterioration, or neglected condition.

Examples:
- B1 Interior Restoration
- B2 Exterior Restoration
- B3 Full Restoration

## C — SPECIALTY / ADD-ONS

Targeted services performed independently or added to a primary service.

Examples:
- C1 Pet Hair Treatment
- C2 Odor Treatment
- C3 Seat / Carpet Extraction
- C4 Headlight Restoration
- C5 Engine Bay Detail
- C6 Other Specialty

These are service definitions, not prices.

---

# 2. UNIVERSAL SERVICE SPECIFICATION

Every service added to the catalog must contain these fields:

1. **Purpose** — What problem the service is intended to solve.
2. **Applicable Conditions** — Which vehicle conditions the service is appropriate for.
3. **Included Procedures** — Exactly what production work is included.
4. **Exclusions** — Work that is not included and requires separate classification or quote.
5. **Required Equipment** — Tools/equipment required to perform the service safely and consistently.
6. **Required Materials** — Chemicals, consumables, towels, pads, bags, etc. required or commonly consumed.
7. **Labor Target** — Target production time or measurable labor unit. Initial values remain field-data driven.
8. **QC Standard** — What must be verified before the service can be marked complete.

No service is considered fully defined until all eight fields exist.

---

# 3. INITIAL SERVICE CATALOG

## A1 — MAINTENANCE INTERIOR

**Purpose**
Maintain and improve a generally serviceable interior through routine cleaning.

**Applicable Conditions**
Primarily C1. May be used on C2 only when the actual scope remains within maintenance-level labor.

**Included Procedures**
- Interior inspection
- Remove loose debris
- Clean accessible interior surfaces
- Clean common touch points
- Vacuum accessible carpet, seats, mats, and floor areas
- Interior glass cleaning
- Final interior inspection

**Exclusions**
- Severe stain restoration
- Heavy pet-hair removal
- Biohazard cleanup
- Mold remediation
- Deep extraction unless specifically included in the approved quote
- Repair of damaged materials

**Required Equipment**
Defined from TAUR's active equipment inventory and validated in field use.

**Required Materials**
Defined from TAUR's active chemical/consumable inventory.

**Labor Target**
To be established from completed-job measurements.

**QC Standard**
All accessible included areas are inspected, visibly addressed, and documented exceptions are recorded.

---

## A2 — MAINTENANCE EXTERIOR

**Purpose**
Maintain an already serviceable exterior through routine cleaning.

**Applicable Conditions**
Primarily C1. C2 requires scope confirmation.

**Included Procedures**
- Exterior condition inspection
- Exterior wash process appropriate to vehicle condition
- Wheel / tire cleaning within approved scope
- Exterior glass cleaning
- Drying and final inspection

**Exclusions**
- Paint correction
- Heavy oxidation removal
- Deep tar, sap, or bonded-contaminant restoration unless quoted
- Paint repair
- Body repair

**Required Equipment**
Defined from active TAUR equipment inventory.

**Required Materials**
Defined from active TAUR chemical/consumable inventory.

**Labor Target**
To be established from completed-job measurements.

**QC Standard**
Included exterior surfaces, wheels/tires, and glass are inspected after completion; remaining defects or excluded contamination are documented.

---

## A3 — MAINTENANCE FULL

**Purpose**
Routine combined interior and exterior maintenance.

**Applicable Conditions**
Primarily C1. C2 requires inspection to confirm the work remains maintenance-level.

**Included Procedures**
- A1 Maintenance Interior scope
- A2 Maintenance Exterior scope
- Combined final inspection

**Exclusions**
All exclusions from A1 and A2 unless separately approved.

**Required Equipment**
Equipment required by the included A1/A2 procedures.

**Required Materials**
Materials required by the included A1/A2 procedures.

**Labor Target**
To be established from completed-job measurements.

**QC Standard**
Interior and exterior QC requirements are both satisfied before completion.

---

## B1 — INTERIOR RESTORATION

**Purpose**
Address interior contamination or deterioration requiring materially more labor than routine maintenance.

**Applicable Conditions**
C2-C3 after inspection. C4 requires inspection and scope approval before fixed pricing.

**Included Procedures**
Defined per inspected vehicle and approved scope. May include deeper cleaning processes beyond A1.

**Exclusions**
- Unapproved restoration work
- Material repair/replacement
- Biohazard or hazardous contamination unless specifically approved and safely equipped
- Mold remediation unless separately scoped

**Required Equipment**
Selected according to the inspected condition and approved procedures.

**Required Materials**
Selected according to contamination type and approved procedures.

**Labor Target**
Measured per job; restoration work is expected to be more labor-variable than maintenance work.

**QC Standard**
Approved restoration areas are inspected against the pre-service condition and documented remaining defects.

---

## B2 — EXTERIOR RESTORATION

**Purpose**
Address exterior contamination or condition requiring additional labor beyond routine maintenance.

**Applicable Conditions**
C2-C3 after inspection. C4 requires inspection and scope approval before fixed pricing.

**Included Procedures**
Defined from inspection and approved scope. Procedures may address contamination or buildup beyond routine exterior cleaning.

**Exclusions**
- Body repair
- Paint repair
- Unapproved paint correction
- Permanent correction of defects outside the approved scope

**Required Equipment**
Selected according to inspected condition and approved procedures.

**Required Materials**
Selected according to contamination and approved procedures.

**Labor Target**
Measured from actual jobs.

**QC Standard**
Approved exterior restoration areas are inspected and remaining defects are documented.

---

## B3 — FULL RESTORATION

**Purpose**
Combined interior and exterior restoration for vehicles requiring substantial additional labor.

**Applicable Conditions**
C2-C3 after inspection. C4 requires inspection before fixed scope/pricing.

**Included Procedures**
Combination of approved B1 and B2 procedures.

**Exclusions**
Any work outside the approved B1/B2 scope.

**Required Equipment**
Equipment required by the approved restoration procedures.

**Required Materials**
Materials required by the approved restoration procedures.

**Labor Target**
Established from measured job labor; do not assume a universal restoration time.

**QC Standard**
All approved interior and exterior restoration areas pass their respective QC checks.

# 4. SPECIALTY / ADD-ON SERVICES

## C1 — PET HAIR TREATMENT

**Purpose**
Remove or materially reduce pet hair beyond routine vacuuming.

**Applicable Conditions**
C2-C4 depending on amount, location, material, and labor required.

**Included Procedures**
Inspection, hair-removal process appropriate to the vehicle/materials, collection of removed hair, and final inspection.

**Exclusions**
Damage repair, replacement of damaged upholstery/carpet, and contamination outside the approved scope.

**Required Equipment**
Validated hair-removal tools and vacuum system appropriate to the job.

**Required Materials**
Approved consumables used during the hair-removal process.

**Labor Target**
Measured by actual labor time and severity.

**QC Standard**
Targeted areas inspected; remaining hair or inaccessible areas documented.

---

## C2 — ODOR TREATMENT

**Purpose**
Treat an identified odor source or reduce persistent odor within an approved scope.

**Applicable Conditions**
C2-C4 depending on source and severity.

**Included Procedures**
Odor-source inspection, appropriate cleaning/treatment steps, and post-treatment verification.

**Exclusions**
Guarantees of permanent odor elimination when the source cannot be fully identified or removed; hazardous remediation unless separately scoped.

**Required Equipment**
Equipment selected according to the approved treatment method.

**Required Materials**
Treatment-specific consumables.

**Labor Target**
Measured by treatment time and required preparation.

**QC Standard**
Treatment completed according to scope and outcome recorded, including any residual odor.

---

## C3 — SEAT / CARPET EXTRACTION

**Purpose**
Deep-clean fabric surfaces where routine cleaning is insufficient.

**Applicable Conditions**
C2-C4 after inspection.

**Included Procedures**
Inspection, appropriate extraction process, drying/ventilation procedure, and final inspection.

**Exclusions**
Permanent dye damage, burns, tears, material failure, or stains that cannot be safely treated.

**Required Equipment**
Validated extraction equipment and supporting tools.

**Required Materials**
Appropriate extraction chemistry and consumables.

**Labor Target**
Measured by treated area and actual labor time.

**QC Standard**
Treated surfaces inspected for coverage, remaining staining, moisture concerns, and documented limitations.

---

## C4 — HEADLIGHT RESTORATION

**Purpose**
Improve clarity of degraded exterior headlight lenses through an approved restoration process.

**Applicable Conditions**
Only after inspection confirms the lens condition is suitable for the selected process.

**Included Procedures**
Inspection, approved restoration process, finishing process, and final visual inspection.

**Exclusions**
Replacement of damaged lamps/housings, internal failure, cracked lenses, or repair beyond the approved restoration scope.

**Required Equipment**
Equipment appropriate to the selected restoration process.

**Required Materials**
Process-specific abrasives, compounds, coatings, and consumables as applicable.

**Labor Target**
Measured from actual completed jobs.

**QC Standard**
Both targeted lenses inspected for clarity, consistency, and completion of the approved process.

---

## C5 — ENGINE BAY DETAIL

**Purpose**
Clean and improve the appearance of accessible engine-bay surfaces within a controlled scope.

**Applicable Conditions**
Inspection required before service.

**Included Procedures**
Inspection, controlled cleaning of approved accessible areas, drying, and final inspection.

**Exclusions**
Mechanical diagnosis/repair, electrical repair, removal of components, hazardous contamination, and cleaning areas that cannot be safely accessed.

**Required Equipment**
Equipment appropriate to the approved low-risk cleaning method.

**Required Materials**
Approved cleaning and protection materials.

**Labor Target**
Measured from actual jobs.

**QC Standard**
Approved accessible areas inspected; excluded or unsafe areas documented.

---

## C6 — OTHER SPECIALTY / ADD-ON

**Purpose**
Capture a specialty service not yet represented by a dedicated catalog item.

**Applicable Conditions**
Inspection required.

**Included Procedures**
Must be explicitly written into the quote before approval.

**Exclusions**
Anything not explicitly included in the approved scope.

**Required Equipment**
Recorded before execution.

**Required Materials**
Recorded before execution.

**Labor Target**
Measured from actual job.

**QC Standard**
Custom QC requirement must be written into the job before completion.

# 5. CONDITION OVERRIDE RULE

**CONDITION BEATS VEHICLE TYPE.**

Vehicle size determines base capacity. Condition determines labor requirement.

Therefore:

- C1 → maintenance workflow is generally appropriate.
- C2 → inspect for additional labor before quoting.
- C3 → restoration scope should be expected and documented.
- C4 → inspection is mandatory before fixed scope/pricing.

A large clean vehicle is not automatically more labor-intensive than a small heavily contaminated vehicle.

---

# 6. PRODUCTION DATA REQUIRED FROM EVERY COMPLETED JOB

For each service, TAUR should capture:

- Service ID
- Vehicle size
- Condition
- Quoted amount
- Final amount
- Labor hours
- Travel minutes
- Materials cost
- Add-ons
- QC completion
- Problems encountered
- Remaining defects
- Customer reaction

This data is the feedback loop for future pricing and service design.

---

# 7. CATALOG GOVERNANCE

A service may be changed only by updating its defined production scope and version.

Do not silently change what a service includes while retaining the same historical definition.

Future catalog versions should preserve:

- Service ID
- Version
- Effective date
- Definition changes
- Reason for change

Historical jobs must retain the service definition/version used when they were sold.
